# Brand Direction & UI Design System

## 1. Brand Identity: Warp Ladger 🔷
- **Tone**: Trustworthy, precise, modern, crisp, professional, and audit-ready.
- **Personality**: Financial rigor meets modern computational intelligence.

## 2. Color Palette & Theming
- **Primary Accent**: Deep Cobalt / Electric Royal (`#1E40AF` / `#2563EB`) representing banking stability and digital speed.
- **Surface / Background**:
  - Canvas: Pure slate / off-white (`#F8FAFC`) for maximum readability.
  - Card Surfaces: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with subtle border elevation (`#E2E8F0`).
  - Dark Elements: Deep Charcoal (`#0F172A`).
- **Semantic Accents**:
  - Success / Paid: Emerald (`#059669` / `#10B981`)
  - Pending / Warning: Amber (`#D97706` / `#F59E0B`)
  - Destructive / Overdue: Crimson (`#DC2626` / `#EF4444`)
  - Smart AI Indicator: Indigo / Purple gradient (`#6366F1` to `#8B5CF6`)

## 3. Typography & Micro-Interactions
- **Font Stack**: `Inter`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `Segoe UI`, `Roboto`, sans-serif.
- **Numerical Figures**: Monospace or tabular numerals (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) for all financial amounts, invoice totals, balances, and dates to ensure clean vertical alignment.
- **Micro-Animations**: Fast transitions (150ms-200ms cubic-bezier) for state changes, loading indicators, and toast alerts.
