# Commercial & Intellectual Property (IP) Policy

## 1. Ownership Charter

| Asset Dimension | Legal Owner | Governance & Policy |
|---|---|---|
| **Source Code** | Warp Ladger Limited | Proprietary closed-source commercial software. All rights reserved. |
| **Product & IP** | Warp Ladger Limited | Trademarks, patents, trade secrets, software architecture, algorithms, UI designs, and database models. |
| **Domain & Brand Assets** | Warp Ladger Limited | Root domains (`warpladger.com`, `warpladger.co.uk`), logos, iconography, visual assets, and marketing collateral. |
| **Customer Financial Data** | Customer / Client Business | The customer retains 100% legal ownership of their business records, contacts, ledger entries, files, and transaction data. Warp Ladger acts purely as a Data Processor under UK GDPR / EU GDPR. |
| **AI Generated Artifacts & Extractions** | Customer / Client Business | Structured extraction data (OCR text, invoice totals, field mappings) derived from customer-uploaded documents belongs entirely to the customer tenant. |
| **Model Weights & Platform Training** | Warp Ladger Limited | Base models and anonymized benchmark heuristics remain platform property. Customer private documents are **never** used to train public foundation models without explicit contractual opt-in. |

---

## 2. Licensing & Commercial Terms
- **Subscription License**: SaaS recurring subscription per organisation, tiered by feature entitlements, storage volume, and processing units.
- **Data Portability**: Customers have the right at any time to export their entire ledger, contacts, journals, and attached documents in standardized formats (CSV, JSON, PDF bundle).
- **Data Isolation Guarantee**: No customer data is co-mingled in application memory across tenant requests.
