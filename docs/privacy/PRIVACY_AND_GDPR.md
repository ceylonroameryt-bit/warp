# Privacy, GDPR & Data Retention Policy

## 1. Regulatory Framework
Warp Ladger complies with the UK Data Protection Act 2018 and the General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR / EU GDPR).

## 2. Roles & Data Classifications
- **Data Controller**: The subscribing business/organisation is the Data Controller of customer, supplier, invoice, and transactional personal data.
- **Data Processor**: Warp Ladger operates as the Data Processor under Article 28 GDPR.
- **Data Subject Rights**:
  - **Right to Access**: Export tools providing complete ledger, invoice, and contact archives in structured formats (JSON, CSV).
  - **Right to Rectification**: Contact information, user profiles, and draft documents can be corrected by authorized users.
  - **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")**: User accounts can be soft/hard-deleted. **Exception**: Financial transactions and statutory accounting books must be retained for the legally mandated period (e.g. 6 years under UK HMRC statutory regulations).

## 3. Data Retention Schedule

| Category | Retention Duration | Justification |
|---|---|---|
| Active Tenant Records | Duration of subscription | Operational SaaS service delivery |
| Financial Books & Journals | 6 years + current fiscal year | Statutory tax compliance (HMRC / Companies Act) |
| Audit Logs | 7 years | Security forensics and statutory compliance |
| Soft-Deleted Entities | 90 days before cold archive | Accidental deletion recovery buffer |
| Authentication Tokens | Max 7 days (session expiration) | Security hygiene |
